This is the English overview of a Spanish-language course. Class guides and slides are available in English; notebooks and cookbooks are in Spanish and marked (ES).

How the course works

1

Access to models

You create a free NVIDIA Build account and an nvapi- API key. That's all you need for modules 1 to 3.

2

Notebooks in Colab or locally

You build the agent from scratch in Python: loop, ReAct, tools, routing, RAG and subagents. They also run without a key, using a simulated model.

3

A real lab on Brev

From module 4 you launch NemoClaw on a cloud machine: OpenClaw inside an OpenShell sandbox. You operate it from the browser: the lab dashboard gives you the chat, a terminal and the OpenShell monitor.

Every class comes with a step-by-step guide, slides, a notebook or cookbook, and a table of common errors. The slides open with what we learned last class and close with what comes next.

The stack at a glance

OpenClaw

The agent

A runtime with memory, tools, sessions and a file workspace (SOUL.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md, skills/).

OpenShell

The sandbox

Isolates the agent: allowlisted network, Landlock for files, seccomp for syscalls, an unprivileged user. It routes inference without putting your key inside the sandbox.

NemoClaw

The blueprint

NVIDIA's reference stack that installs, configures and operates OpenClaw inside OpenShell, with the nemoclaw CLI and versioned policies.

The errors you'll see most

SymptomLikely causeWhat to do
401 UnauthorizedThe key was copied wrong, has spaces, or was revoked.Copy it in full (it starts with nvapi-) and rerun the setup cell.
403 ForbiddenValid key, but no access to the model or no credits left.Check your account at build.nvidia.com or switch models.
404 / 410The model doesn't exist or was retired from the free endpoint.Copy the exact ID from the catalog; update NVIDIA_MODEL.
429 Too Many RequestsToo many requests per minute.Wait a few seconds; retry with backoff.
finish_reason = lengthThe model reasoned until it ran out of max_tokens.Raise max_tokens (reasoning models need it).
Building your agent / 503The agent is still being created or starting up.Wait a few minutes without reloading; check in Brev that the instance says Running.
It asks you to sign in againYour NVIDIA session on the lab page expired or you resumed the instance.Click the green NemoClaw button in Brev again and sign in with your NVIDIA account.
Out of credits on BrevOften the wrong organization is active.Switch organizations in Brev before deploying.
