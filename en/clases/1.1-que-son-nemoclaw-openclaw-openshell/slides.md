## How a request flows

:::diagrama flujo-peticion

Notes:
Five clicks: you and the tool; the front desk (gateway) with its key kept outside; the room with the agent; the way out to the model (allowed, the key is added outside); the way out to the internet (blocked except the list). Two ideas: the key never enters the room and the network is closed by default.

## What people often mix up

:::cols
**Myth**
"NemoClaw is an NVIDIA model"
|||
**Reality**
NemoClaw is the **kit** that builds the room
The models are called **Nemotron** and you use them from build.nvidia.com
:::

:::tip
OpenShell works without NemoClaw. NemoClaw sets it up securely, and it updates very often: in the lab we use a pinned version.
:::
