If something goes wrong

You seeDo this
The label “facturacion” isn't on the listExpected: one retry, then “otro”
The answer comes back cut off or without the formGive the answer more room (max_tokens)
Error 400 when asking for the fixed-field formContinue without the form, but still check
Error 429 in a branchFewer branches at once and retry with a wait
The program crashes looking for the windowCheck the label before sending the ticket
